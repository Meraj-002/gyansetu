Flashcard images
================

Every image bundled here was checked at download time to be in the public
domain or released under CC0, from Wikimedia Commons. Neither licence requires
attribution; this file is kept as a record.

They are downloaded once and shipped with the app. Nothing is fetched at
runtime, which is what lets the flashcards work with no connection.

The Numbers cards carry no photograph. A numeral is not a thing that can be
photographed, so those cards draw the digit with that many counters, using the
same shapes the printable worksheet uses.
